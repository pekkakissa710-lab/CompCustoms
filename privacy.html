Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use CompCustoms and sign in with your Epic Games account, we collect limited information required to provide our services. This includes your Epic Games display name and unique account identifier (ID).

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate your identity and grant access to custom tournament keys.
  • Display your username in competitive lobbies and leaderboards.
  • Ensure fair play and enforce platform rules.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with third parties. Authentication is handled securely via Epic Games OAuth API. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Epic Games Inc.

4. Cookies and Storage

We use local storage in your browser to maintain your session state (such as keeping you logged in). No tracking cookies are used for advertising purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our support page or contact us at compcustoms.my.to/support.
